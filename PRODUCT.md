# Nikko autumn trip
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, selected within the delegated implementation scope for GitHub Pages and offline reading.

## Users
Two adults travelling without a car, with limited walking and little interest in temples. Primarily used on a phone while travelling.

## Product Purpose
Put the October 30–November 1, 2026 trip's times, transport, gentle sightseeing and packing list within easy reach.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Public GitHub Pages publication explicitly approved. Never include names, seat numbers, booking codes, payment details or private email links. Clearly distinguish confirmed trains/hotel from proposed activities and unconfirmed shuttle. Save packing state only on the device. Label dated forecasts and scheduled bus arrivals. Offline content is best effort; external maps need connectivity.

## Evidence on Hand
The supplied verified trip brief and official source links. Wikimedia destination photographs with CC BY-SA attribution.

## Product Principles
- Make the next transport decision obvious.
- Preserve a relaxed pace and short walks.
- Show uncertainty beside the affected plan.
- Keep the page lightweight and usable outdoors.
