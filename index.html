Friday · Arrive & unwind

Trade the city for lake air. Today’s only real goal: get comfortable.

  1. 11:30
    Confirmed

    All aboard Kegon 021

    Leave Asakusa. Arrive at Tobu-Nikko at 13:17. Have your express ticket and ordinary rail fare or valid pass ready.

  2. 13:35
    Proposed bus

    Up to Lake Chuzenji

    From bay 2A, take the YK bus toward Yumoto. Get off at stop 29, Chuzenji Yubinkyoku, scheduled 14:27. The hotel is about a minute away.

    Want a lunch break first? The 14:13 bus is scheduled to arrive at 15:05. Allow for autumn delays.

    Bus details & Japanese stop name →
  3. 15:00
    Confirmed stay

    Settle into Yutorelo

    Check in to your non-smoking, 34m² Japanese-Western lake-view room. Advance procedures are complete: save your private check-in email / QR offline and have it ready at the hotel tablet. Confirm dinner and breakfast sittings, especially Sunday’s early start.

  4. Later

    A little lake, a long soak

    Optional 5–10 minute lakeside look, then onsen and your included steak dinner. Finish outdoor exploring around 16:00.

One small job at check-in

Saturday · Waterfall, mountain & lake

Your breakfast is booked for 08:30. Visit Kegon Falls first, then use the late-morning G bus for the roadside viewpoint. Protect the 13:30 cruise; cycling or a Ryuzu Falls streamside hike is optional if weather and buses allow.

  1. 08:30–09:10
    Breakfast time booked

    Breakfast at Yutorelo

    Enjoy breakfast without rushing. Wear warm layers and waterproof shoes; it can be close to freezing at lake level. The earlier 09:00 mountain bus is no longer part of the plan.

  2. 09:30–10:30
    Main waterfall

    Kegon Falls and elevator

    Travel from the hotel to Chuzenji Onsen, then walk to 華厳の滝 (Kegon Falls). See the free upper viewpoint, and, if operating, take the elevator to the dramatic lower viewing platform. Allow time to reach the bus terminal afterward.

    The elevator typically operates 08:00–17:00 in October; ¥600 per adult. Check today's operation.

    Kegon Falls details ↗
  3. By 11:00
    Bus boarding

    Get to Chuzenji Onsen bus terminal · 中禅寺温泉

    Find the seasonal G bus toward Hangetsuyama (半月山), not the Y/YK bus for Yumoto or the bus downhill to Nikko. Aim for the 11:10 departure. Show the driver 中禅寺湖展望台 (Chuzenjiko Tenbodai), the roadside observation deck stop, and confirm the return service before boarding.

    G route runs 1 Oct–8 Nov, but has few departures; check official stop-by-stop departure and return times and any service disruption. If the bus is delayed or the return timing is unsuitable, skip the viewpoint rather than risk the cruise.

    Official departure stop / G timetable ↗
  4. 11:25–11:45
    planned
    20-minute photo stop

    Chuzenjiko Observation Deck

    Exit at 中禅寺湖展望台 (approximately 11:25 if running to schedule). Enjoy the roadside panorama of Lake Chuzenji and Mt. Nantai. For the return, use the G bus toward 中禅寺温泉 (Chuzenji Onsen), targeted for 11:45, arriving back at around 12:00.

    Check both times at the stop: 11:25 and 11:45 are planned times, not guaranteed live arrivals. This is the roadside overlook, not the higher Hangetsuyama summit viewpoint (which requires a hike). Do not walk down the mountain road.

    Official observation deck return stop ↗
  5. 12:00–13:10

    Lunch & transfer to pier

    Have lunch near Chuzenji Onsen, then head to Funenoeki Chuzenji (船の駅中禅寺). Aim to be there by 13:15 to confirm sailing and buy tickets. Keep lunch quick if the mountain bus runs late.

  6. 13:30–14:25
    Published sailing · weather permitting

    55-minute Lake Chuzenji sightseeing cruise

    Take the 13:30 round-trip cruise from Funenoeki Chuzenji, returning around 14:25. Views of Mt. Nantai, golden forests, and the embassy shoreline. The operator also lists some special departures on 31 October, but prioritize this regular sailing.

    Cruises can be cancelled due to wind, visibility or water conditions; confirm the service at the pier.

    Official cruise timetable ↗
  7. 14:45–16:00
    optional
    Only if dry + rental confirmed

    Short lakeside bicycle ride

    If both of you can rent bikes in the afternoon, ride the accessible northern/eastern lakeside roads toward Lake Chuzenji Boathouse and return the same way. Keep this a short out-and-back with photo stops — there is no easy continuous cycle loop around the lake.

    Check first: call Chuzenji Pension (+81 288-55-0888) to ask whether two bicycles are available from 14:45 until about 16:00 and where pickup/return happens. Published rental price has been around ¥500 per hour, but current stock, hours and price are not confirmed. Use lights if needed, avoid slippery roads, and finish well before dusk. If cold/raining or no bikes: substitute a lakeside walk or go straight to the onsen.

    Bicycle rental information ↗
  8. 14:45–16:30
    optional alternative
    Streamside walk · bus-dependent

    Ryuzu Falls (竜頭ノ滝) — short river hike

    Instead of cycling or the embassy villas, take a Yumoto Onsen-bound bus from the Chuzenji area to Ryuzu-no-taki (竜頭の滝). Visit the waterfall viewpoint, then walk upstream beside the Yugawa River on the roughly 300 m cascade-side path. Turn back when ready; allow around 25–40 minutes for the short walk and photos.

    Important: this is an alternative, not an extra guaranteed stop. The bus travel and wait times may make a 16:30 hotel return impossible after the 14:25 cruise. Before leaving, check the downhill return bus toward Chuzenji Onsen/Nikko and your hotel dinner time. Only do it if the schedule works; otherwise enjoy the lake near your hotel. The trail can be wet and slippery, and Ryuzu foliage usually peaks earlier than the lake.

    Check Tobu bus schedules ↗
  9. By 16:30

    Back at Yutorelo · Onsen & dinner

    Return bikes on time, get warm, enjoy your onsen and included premium steak dinner. Confirm Sunday's 08:55 downhill bus from stop 29 before bed so your 12:55 SPACIA X train remains protected.

The bus plan at a glance

11:10 — depart 中禅寺温泉 on G for 半月山.
~11:25 — get off 中禅寺湖展望台.
~11:45 — G toward 中禅寺温泉.
~12:00 — back near lunch and cruise.

Check both directions on the official timetable; peak foliage traffic, weather or road restrictions may change plans. If mountain buses don't line up, keep Kegon Falls and the 13:30 cruise instead. If the cruise is cancelled, consider a café or Nikko Natural Science Museum. Skip bikes in rain, ice or high winds. Akechidaira Ropeway remains closed.

Sunday · Leave the lake early

Protect the train connection first. Any spare time becomes a relaxed brunch in town.

  1. Early

    Breakfast & bags ready

    Arrange breakfast timing with the hotel on Saturday. Aim to check out between 08:20 and 08:30.

  2. 08:40
    Recommended target

    Be at stop 29

    Wait on the downhill side toward Tobu-Nikko for the 08:55 bus. Scheduled station arrival is 09:41.

    The 07:55 bus (08:41 scheduled arrival) gives more buffer. Confirm the correct stop side with the hotel.

  3. In town

    Brunch if you have time

    Stay near Tobu-Nikko station. Keep the morning open; the traffic buffer matters more than another attraction.

  4. 12:30

    Be train-ready

    Have your express ticket and base fare or pass ready, find your platform, and board comfortably.

  5. 12:55
    Confirmed
