Short walks · No rushing

Friday · Arrive & unwind

Trade the city for lake air. Today’s only real goal: get comfortable.

  1. 11:30
    Confirmed

    All aboard Kegon 021

    Leave Asakusa. Arrive at Tobu-Nikko at 13:17. Have your express ticket and ordinary rail fare or valid pass ready.

  2. 13:35
    Proposed bus

    Up to Lake Chuzenji

    From bay 2A, take the YK bus toward Yumoto. Get off at stop 29, Chuzenji Yubinkyoku, scheduled 14:27. The hotel is about a minute away.

    Want a lunch break first? The 14:13 bus is scheduled to arrive at 15:05. Allow for autumn delays.

    Bus details & Japanese stop name →
  3. 15:00
    Confirmed stay

    Settle into Yutorelo

    Check in to your non-smoking, 34m² Japanese-Western lake-view room. Advance procedures are complete: save your private check-in email / QR offline and have it ready at the hotel tablet. Confirm dinner and breakfast sittings, especially Sunday’s early start.

  4. Later

    A little lake, a long soak

    Optional 5–10 minute lakeside look, then onsen and your included steak dinner. Finish outdoor exploring around 16:00.

One small job at check-in

Ask about early Sunday breakfast and the downhill bus stop. The hotel shuttle is not reserved; your enquiry was only a request for its schedule.

Saturday · Lake & waterfall

The scenery does the work. Keep the day flexible and leave space for the onsen.

  1. Morning

    A slow breakfast

    Breakfast at the hotel, then check the weather and cruise operation. Layer up: it feels colder on the water.

  2. 10:00
    or 11:00
    Suggested outing

    See the lake from the water

    Take the 55-minute circuit from Funenoeki Chuzenji. Stay aboard for the full loop; no walking stops needed. Use local transport to reduce the walk to the pier.

    Weather can cancel sailings. Check the day’s service before heading out.

    Cruise timetable ↗
  3. Midday

    Lunch, then a proper break

    Eat around the lake or Chuzenji Onsen. There’s no need to squeeze another sight into the gap.

  4. Afternoon
    Optional outing

    Kegon Falls, at your pace

    Use local transport toward Chuzenji Onsen. The elevator route still involves stairs; the free upper viewpoint also has a sloped approach. Decide what feels comfortable on arrival.

    Elevator: 08:00–17:00 · ¥600 per adult. Recheck operating information before visiting.

    Falls visitor information ↗
  5. By 16:00

    Back to the warm part

    Rest, onsen, dinner. Prepare your small bags and confirm early breakfast for tomorrow.

If the weather turns

Swap the cruise for a café, hotel time or the Nikko Natural Science Museum near Chuzenji Onsen.

Museum: 09:00–17:00, last admission 16:30. Check current operation.

Museum information ↗

Sunday · Leave the lake early

Protect the train connection first. Any spare time becomes a relaxed brunch in town.

  1. Early

    Breakfast & bags ready

    Arrange breakfast timing with the hotel on Saturday. Aim to check out between 08:20 and 08:30.

  2. 08:40
    Recommended target

    Be at stop 29

    Wait on the downhill side toward Tobu-Nikko for the 08:55 bus. Scheduled station arrival is 09:41.

    The 07:55 bus (08:41 scheduled arrival) gives more buffer. Confirm the correct stop side with the hotel.

  3. In town

    Brunch if you have time

    Stay near Tobu-Nikko station. Keep the morning open; the traffic buffer matters more than another attraction.

  4. 12:30

    Be train-ready

    Have your express ticket and base fare or pass ready, find your platform, and board comfortably.

  5. 12:55
    Confirmed

    SPACIA X 006 to Tokyo

    Tobu-Nikko → Asakusa. Arrive at 14:45.

Before you board

  • Express tickets ≠ ordinary fare. Your base rail fare or suitable pass still needs checking.
  • No bus reservation or guaranteed seat. Keep luggage small.
  • Suica / PASMO: tap on and off. With cash, take the numbered slip and pay when exiting.
  • Hotel shuttle: unconfirmed. The 15:00 Tobu-Nikko pickup needs advance confirmation.
